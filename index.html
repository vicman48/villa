About Me

VILLASEÑOR

As a DJ, Producer, promoter and label manager behind Vigor Trax Villaseñor stands as a true trailblazer. Notability, he is the main resident of the esteemed Pervert collective

With over 14 years behind the decks, his impeccable techniquem electric sessions and energetic productions filled with groove have positioned him as one of foremost figures in the Mexican electronic music scene. With performances in the most prestigious clubs and festivals in Mexico, he has/had the opportunity to play at the most important parties and clubs in cities such as Berlin, Amsterdam, Barcelona, New York City, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Vancouver, Buenos Aires, Sao Paulo, Santiago, Medellín, Bogotá, San José, etc...

Among, the artists with whom he has shared the booth are names such as: Boris, Dax J, Etapp Kyle, Akua, Nastia, Objekt, Anetha, Metaraph, Insolate, Norbak, MXMLXXXV, Cem, Roi Pérez, Matrixxman, Ur Trax, Eris Drew, Aadja, Carry Nation, Volvox, Cashu, Sol Ortega, Luis Flores among others.
